## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of scientific clinical authority and nurturing, human-centered warmth. Tailored for parents navigating sensitive developmental stages, educators seeking evidence-based behavioral strategies, and institutional leadership commissioning workshops, the interface must project absolute psychological safety, clarity, and seasoned expertise.

The design movement blends **Warm Editorial** with **Tactile / Soft Minimalism**:
- **Human & Dignified:** High-contrast editorial display typography signals research rigor, literary care, and thought leadership without feeling cold or clinical.
- **Supportive & Grounded:** Abundant, intentional whitespace gives breathing room to emotional and high-stakes content. Dense cognitive loads are disassembled into digestible, quiet modules.
- **Gentle Reassurance:** Softened geometry, gentle organic tonalities, and physical, paper-like tactile depth eliminate clinical sterility in favor of a quiet, reassuring consultation space.

## Colors

The palette grounds users in calming biophilic tones paired with warm earth pigments:

- **Primary (`#4A6B5D` - Calming Sage Green):** Evokes emotional regulation, equilibrium, and growth. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active navigational states, and authoritative milestones.
- **Secondary (`#C87D55` - Soft Terracotta / Warm Sand):** Infuses emotional warmth, human presence, and empathy. Reserved for secondary actions, developmental stage markers, and highlight callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B43` - Warm Amber):** Delivers gentle reassurance, illuminating key guidance tips, notification badges, and developmental milestones without alarmism.
- **Neutral (`#263238` - Deep Slate):** Provides high-contrast, deeply legible text rendering that avoids the harshness of pure black.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background canvases use warm parchment creams (`#FAF8F5`) transitioning into crisp paper whites (`#FFFFFF`) for layered card elevation, preventing screen glare.

## Typography

The type scale balances academic discernment with welcoming readability:

- **Display & Section Headers (`Newsreader`):** An optical editorial serif that communicates depth, clinical authority, and literary warmth. Its organic serifs counteract digital fatigue, offering an approachable, human voice for essays, course titles, and consultation headers. Italic variations should be applied deliberately to key emotional concepts or empathic pull-quotes.
- **Body & UI Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A geometric-humanist sans-serif that remains friendly and distinct even at micro-scales. Generous aperture and clear differentiation between glyphs allow exhausted parents and busy educators to consume long-form behavioral guides effortlessly.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible, responsive 12-column grid layout designed for high scannability and focused reading:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Text containers for essays and clinical descriptions should cap at an optimal reading width of 680px (8 columns maximum).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Resource sidebars shift into stacked drawers or tabbed groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Complex booking flows and developmental stage matrices collapse into vertical linear journeys.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical component gaps (`space-xl`) separate thematic content to prevent visual overwhelm, fostering a peaceful user environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through paper-like tonal layering, warmth-tinted ambient shadows, and gentle borders:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Canvas:* Deep cream background (`#FAF8F5`) providing a calming base.
  - *Resting Surfaces (Cards, Modules):* Crisp soft-white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate outline (`rgba(74, 107, 93, 0.08)`) establishing immediate separation without abrupt contrast.
  - *Floating Dialogs & Popovers:* White surfaces layered over soft blurred backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`).
- **Shadow Philosophy:** Shadows are ultra-diffuse and tinted with deep slate and sage pigment rather than neutral black:
  - *Level 1 (Cards, Rest):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(74, 107, 93, 0.03)`
  - *Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 107, 93, 0.04)`
  - *Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Diagnostic Overlays):* `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a softened, rounded profile (`level 2`, base `0.5rem` / `8px`):
- Standard interactive elements like inputs, dropdowns, and button containers use `0.5rem` radius to convey approachability while maintaining professional structure.
- Content containers, parent guides, and workshop cards use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to create soft, inviting frames.
- Reassurance tags, developmental stage badges, and alert banners use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) or full pill styling to eliminate harsh edges in emotionally sensitive touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Calming Sage green background (`#4A6B5D`) with pure white text, styled with `title-md` or `label-md` weight. Minimum height of 48px to ensure calm, accessible interaction on touchscreens. Hover transitions seamlessly into a richer forest hue (`#3B564A`) with subtle vertical lift.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in Terracotta (`#C87D55`) and text in `#C87D55`. Hover fills with a delicate 6% terracotta wash (`rgba(200, 125, 85, 0.06)`).
- **Tertiary / Subtle Actions:** Unbordered, deep slate text with subtle sage-tinted underline that activates on hover.

### Reassurance Badges & Chips
- Designed with full-pill geometry (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-padded labels indicating age groups (e.g., *Ages 4-7*), clinical validity (e.g., *Evidence-Based Framework*), or audience type (e.g., *Educator Workshop*).
- Colors leverage low-saturation pastel tints with deep slate text:
  - Sage badge: Background `rgba(74, 107, 93, 0.12)`, text `#334C41`.
  - Terracotta badge: Background `rgba(200, 125, 85, 0.12)`, text `#8E4F2D`.
  - Amber badge: Background `rgba(217, 155, 67, 0.14)`, text `#805615`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background filled with neutral off-white surface, framed by a soft border (`rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.15)`).
- Generous padding (`space-md`) ensuring effortless form completion for consultations and triage questionnaires.
- Focus state switches border to Sage Green (`#4A6B5D`) accompanied by a subtle 3px sage glow (`rgba(74, 107, 93, 0.15)`).

### Cards & Content Modules
- Rendered on crisp white surfaces against the cream canvas with `1rem` corner rounding.
- Clear internal padding scale: `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on mobile expanding to `space-xl` (`2rem`) on desktop.
- Consultation booking cards and resource modules feature distinct tinted header banners (e.g., soft sage wash) to visually separate metadata from content.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and rounded checkboxes (`4px` inner radius) sized at `20px` to maintain a human, friendly touch.
- Selected state fills in Sage Green with a clean white micro-icon check.
