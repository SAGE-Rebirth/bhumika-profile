Meet Your Consultant: Where Scientific Inquiry Meets Empathetic Listening

My path began inside active learning spaces. Having served as a Mathematics Subject Matter Expert and frontline educator, I witnessed firsthand how quickly a bright child’s natural inquisitiveness can shut down when met with rigid pedagogical expectations or misunderstood emotional overwhelm.

At the Institute for Excellence in Higher Education (IEHE), Bhopal, my ongoing academic journey in Psychology, combined with a minor in Sociology, provides a uniquely multidimensional lens: we don’t view a child’s struggles in isolation. We examine systemic dynamics—household routines, social environments, school culture, and individualized neurological processing.

My goal is simple yet uncompromising: to translate clinical research into practical, loving, daily routines that restore confidence for children and peace of mind for parents and educators.

Real Case Scenarios & Impact

De-identified transformations showcasing our empathetic, evidence-based methodology in practice.

Grade 4 • Bhopal auto_graph

Overcoming Math Anxiety & School Resistance

Context: A 9-year-old student developed severe morning stomachaches and tears before math periods.

Intervention: Deconstructed concept overload using visual representations and paired parents with a 10-minute low-stakes math game routine.

Outcome: Zero somatic complaints after 3 weeks; student volunteered to solve board problems in class.
Age 7 • Virtual psychology

Regulation Routines for a Highly Sensitive Child

Context: Daily post-school meltdowns lasting 45+ minutes, leaving working parents exhausted and reactive.

Intervention: Implemented a 20-minute sensory decompression transition station before any conversation or homework demands.

Outcome: Meltdowns decreased by 80%; child learned to identify early sensory overwhelm cues independently.
School Faculty • Arera Colony groups

Inclusive Classroom Faculty Training

Context: Primary school staff struggling to manage divergent attention spans and sensory seeking behavior without penalizing kids.

Intervention: Delivered a high-engagement 2-day workshop on micro-breaks, fidget integration, and restorative classroom agreements.

Outcome: Disciplinary referrals fell by 42% over the academic term across Grade 1–5 cohorts.
